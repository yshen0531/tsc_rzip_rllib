# R_geo/Z_geo 1 ms post-ID2Z19R1 Authority-L0 route review

## Decision

The next route is not a third predictor and is not a combined
"authority/recovery" claim. It is one bounded, model-free,
simulator-development `Authority-L0 / capture-feasibility` campaign. Its
purpose is to decide whether the exact signed Card15 action grammar can
produce sustained, control-aligned R/Z and terminal-speed correction when the
decision rule uses the exact current R_geo/Z_geo/Ip observation every
millisecond.

Authority-L0, a nominal capture seed and Recourse-L1 are distinct:

```text
Authority-L0
    sustained finite control-aligned action utility

nominal capture seed
    a repeated finite path with six consecutive states inside the frozen
    position / terminal-speed / Ip capture set

Recourse-L1
    a prospectively qualified contingency policy/tree on a bounded tube that
    respects all hard constraints and enters a smaller terminal recoverable set
```

An exact Card15 return, a hold command, a safe stop after a successor, or a
fresh replay does not by itself establish Recourse-L1.

## Evidence behind the decision

ID2Z19R1 is final. Both frozen candidates failed terminal one-step increment
and terminal speed in all eight whole-history folds. Support is not the main
explanation: 190/196 structured and 192/197 TCN R over-cap rows were inside
the frozen support gate. Across 202 eligible true positive-R terminal-
increment rows, both models predicted zero positive increments. The fixed
TCN residual did not repair the event error and failed its relative selection
gate.

ID2Z18 proves a different finite fact. Its signed histories separated in R/Z
by 2.715--7.464 mm, so the exact actions have measurable plant effect.
Nevertheless all fourteen fit histories contain zero states satisfying the
joint 25 mm / 0.1 m/s / 5% Ip capture condition. The full-F reference passed
through 23.207 mm / 0.218 m/s at state 48 but its terminal window deteriorated
to about 28.255 mm / 0.416 m/s. The missing result is therefore sustained,
target-aligned authority and capture, not generic response signal or input
rank.

## One bounded next campaign

The next design will use one prospectively frozen sequential campaign with a
hard maximum near 23 rollouts rather than another open-ended macro ladder:

1. one matched moving-nominal path;
2. four exact executed rank-four Card15 coordinates, both signs, at two
   frozen action phases (`1 + 4 * 2 * 2 = 17` development paths);
3. exact cumulative return and a common long tail on every path;
4. one deterministic selector, frozen before results, that may nominate at
   most two low-dimensional feedback or segmented-policy candidates;
5. one selected fresh replay;
6. one prospectively fixed different complete arrival-history root with its
   matched nominal, selected policy and finite fallback continuation.

The selected rule must use only pre-issue exact current R_geo/Z_geo/Ip,
causal takeover history, current/queue state and the frozen target. It may
not use future TSC state, family labels, a matched future hold, post-result
manual action edits or clipping. Every positive and negative complete
development window is declared fit-eligible before execution; selector
replay, different-history validation and fallback evidence have zero fit
weight.

The simulator-development contract permits this one bounded prospective
exposure to finite novel successors under exact interface checks and frozen
outer-envelope aborts. This is not a controller-grade transition tube.

## Authority-L0 and capture gates

Interface, source-prefix, issue/effect, exact Card15, slew, absolute-current,
paired-boundary, Ip, outer-envelope, raw inventory and independent
recomputation are hard gates. Any violation is execution/integrity failure,
not a scientific authority result.

Authority-L0 additionally requires all of the following:

- each admitted signed coordinate has a sustained response over at least
  three non-single-frame states at one frozen phase;
- at least two consecutive common states provide a nonnegative two-axis
  signed correction cone with a prospectively frozen minimum directional
  projection;
- the selected six-state worst capture score improves at least 15% over its
  matched nominal, the worst terminal speed improves by at least 0.05 m/s,
  and distance worsens by no more than 0.5 mm;
- the six tail states are individually no worse under the frozen partial
  order, so a one-frame dip cannot pass;
- the fresh replay and the different-history validation satisfy the same
  finite claim;
- the explicit finite fallback continuation is executed and remains hard
  safe, without being called recovery.

A nominal capture seed requires six consecutive states satisfying distance
at most 25 mm, one-millisecond speed at most 0.1 m/s and Ip deviation at most
5%, plus fresh replay. Authority-L0 without capture may open only a new
event/candidate-value development identity. It does not open a controller.

## Route after the result

```text
Authority-L0 FAIL
    close this signed action/phase grammar;
    redesign action allocation, takeover phase or terminal target;
    do not add model capacity

Authority-L0 PASS without capture
    one new event-phase / candidate-value model-development identity may open;
    controller and Recourse-L1 remain blocked

capture seed PASS
    event/value model work and a separate Recourse-L1 design may proceed in
    parallel

fresh model calibration + blind holdout PASS
AND Recourse-L1 PASS
AND exact hard interface PASS
    first source-local feedback sentinel may be designed
```

The current one-millisecond contract is the simulated plant issue period.
Canonical-source TSC replay is wall-clock slow and is used as a development
teacher/planner unless a later wall-clock requirement changes. It is not an
arbitrary-state snapshot Oracle and does not replace a qualified runtime
model or recovery layer.

## Final goal retained

The final goal remains fixed-1100-ms takeover with exact pre-issue paired-
boundary R_geo/Z_geo and same-step Ip, complete takeover-era causal history,
exact Card15/queue/current constraints and independent safety/recourse, for
safe approximate two-axis finite waypoint/path tracking. Later work must
cover multiple positions and histories, bidirectional repeated R_mid
crossing and continuous belief. Authority-L0 is one source-local admission
gate, not a reduction of that goal.
