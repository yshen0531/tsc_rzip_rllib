# Fixed-1000 absolute radial Authority G0 design

## Purpose

G0 is the first bounded experiment under the post-F1 Authority-first route.
It asks one control-relevant question before any new model is trained:

> Can a sustained exact-Card15 radial allocation, starting at the authentic
> 1000 ms source, materially beat the real matched q0 continuation in both
> terminal source distance and terminal speed?

This is not another response-peak probe.  The decision uses states 56--64 of
a 64 ms trajectory, a fresh matched q0 rollout, absolute source distance,
one-step R/Z speed, Ip and hard current/interface gates.

## Why this is the next experiment

The B0 q0 continuation drifts `23.538658 mm` from the source and reaches a
late maximum speed of `0.318027 m/s`.  D1 established a reproducible signed
two-axis transient response.  N0 then showed that an even-minus ramp can be
about `1.63 mm` better near state16 yet is worse than q0 at the terminal
window.  Therefore neither a short peak nor input rank is Authority.

The opposite sustained sign has not been measured.  D1's returned-pulse tail
has the opposite late sign, so even-plus is a justified prospective
candidate, not a post-result amplitude patch.  G0 freezes all depths at once
and permanently closes this exact depth matrix after one execution.

## Frozen matrix and roles

- one fresh q0 continuation through state64;
- even-plus ramp-from-issue0 candidates at depths 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32,
  each holding its attained exact Card15 level through state64;
- one exact depth-16 replay for execution/repeatability integrity;
- 8 rollouts and at most 512 plant advances; no retry after any attempt.

The q0 and six primary candidates are prospectively development-fit eligible.
The replay is zero-fit.  D1/N0 are consumed design evidence only.  No row is
calibration, blind holdout, controller, expert or Recourse data.

## Gates and route meaning

Every rollout must pass exact 1000 ms restart, paired-boundary R/Z, Ip,
Card15, issued/observed slew, current, limiter, 50 mm R/Z and 10% Ip hard
envelopes.  The fresh q0 must complete before active candidates have a valid
scientific comparator.

A candidate passes the radial utility gate only when its state56--64 window,
relative to the fresh q0 window, simultaneously provides:

- at least 15% reduction in worst source distance;
- at least `0.03 m/s` reduction in maximum one-step R/Z speed;
- terminal maximum `|Ip-Ip_source|/|Ip_source| <= 5%`.

The 5 mm / 0.1 m/s capture thresholds remain diagnostics; G0 need not reach
capture and cannot claim hold or recovery.  A single transient improvement
cannot substitute for the terminal window.

PASS authorizes only a separately frozen moving-allocation co-design around
the selected radial center, including signed Z and neighboring radial
allocations.  It does not authorize a model, calibration, holdout, Recourse
or feedback controller.  FAIL closes this exact even-plus depth matrix and
forces nominal/action-basis redesign before model fitting; no adjacent depth,
amplitude or duration ladder may be appended.
