# R_geo/Z_geo 1 ms ID-2G1 grouped causal model comparison design

Date: 2026-08-17 Asia/Shanghai

Identity: `rgeo-zgeo-1ms-id2g1-grouped-causal-model-comparison-v1`

## Purpose and evidence boundary

ID-2F1R1 completed 78/78 authentic trajectories and its independent raw
audit qualified 39 unique whole-history cells for development fitting.  The
primary/independent compact hashes are
`897d0a8a44c305891d77e29c728d171cd8857869a91d00b3268cac39d6d1e655`
and
`d08c85ec3cd3b027ec2b00f1307c2adf9fb8176d47353ce5a823000d103647e5`.

ID-2G1 runs on the server and makes zero reset, TSC, `gotsc`, controller or
plant calls.  It may read only the immutable ID-2F1R1 raw and compact
evidence.  It must not read ID-2C2, calibration, blind holdout, old probe
data, expert data or controller trajectories.

This stage asks a machine-learning question, not a complete mechanism-
identification question: can a small causal model predict the finite
source-local response distribution across the three held-out complete
history contexts?  Unexplained but reproducible jumps remain prediction
targets; they are not deleted, relabelled as corruption or made a prerequisite
for physical attribution.

## Dataset and causal contract

The two exact replays of each ID-2F1R1 cell are integrity replicates, not
independent statistical samples.  The extractor averages their allowed
numeric fields after rechecking exact replay equality, and assigns each of
the 39 unique cells unit weight.

At issue `k`, the predictor may use:

- current and past exact same-step paired-boundary R_geo/Z_geo and same-step
  Ip from state 0 through state `k`;
- current and past exact 14-coil readback/current values;
- controller-owned issued Card15 actions through issue `k`, including the
  candidate future issued schedule during a rollout;
- the one-ms clock and causal takeover-history mask.

The predictor may not use future plasma state, future actual/readback coil
current, wire current, `sprsina`, `outputa`, future matched baseline,
context/direction/sign/duration labels, evaluator outcome, calibration or
holdout fields.  Wire current remains an audit diagnostic rather than a
deployment feature.  The exact actuator boundary propagates candidate issued
Card15 current; it is not learned by the plasma model.

The 14-dimensional current/action vectors are represented in the frozen
rank-three source-local Card15 span generated by p03, p04 and p07.  Projection
residual must remain at numerical zero for every extracted row.  This is an
explicit finite input domain, not a claim over the full 14-dimensional cube.

## Grouping and predictions

There are exactly three outer leave-one-context-out folds:

1. hold `none`;
2. hold `p04_plus_i18_d1`;
3. hold `p07_plus_i18_d1`.

All baseline and probe cells, both replay members and the complete causal
prefix of a context stay in the same fold.  No step-level random split is
allowed.  Hyperparameters, normalization and early stopping use only the two
training contexts of each outer fold; the held context cannot choose them.

Every candidate is evaluated in three distinct modes:

1. rolling one-step prediction, where the true current state and all prior
   causal history are available at each issue;
2. free recursive rollout from state 16 through state 34, using only the true
   causal prefix through state 16 and the frozen future issued Card15
   schedule;
3. evaluator-only paired response from state 23 through state 34, computed as
   predicted action-cell minus predicted matched baseline and compared with
   the corresponding true difference.  The future baseline is never a model
   input.

The state target is the next increment of `[R_geo, Z_geo, Ip]`.  Current
R_geo/Z_geo/Ip are exact observations, not latent noisy measurements.  Model
uncertainty represents latent history/dynamics and finite-data/model mismatch.

## Frozen candidates

The same folds and metrics compare:

1. `stable_lpv`: a ridge-fitted stable fixed-pole state/history model with
   poles `[0.20, 0.50, 0.80, 0.95]`, current state/velocity, time and frozen
   Card15-span coordinates; this is the simplest eligible backbone;
2. `small_gru`: width 12, one recurrent layer, three fixed seeds, teacher-
   forced one-step training and causal free rollout;
3. `causal_tcn`: width 12, causal dilations `[1, 2, 4, 8]`, three fixed seeds
   and a 16-frame receptive field;
4. `probabilistic_ensemble`: the equal-weight predictive mixture of the
   retained GRU/TCN members and the structured member, with a training-only
   residual floor.  Its intervals are development diagnostics and are not
   called calibrated tubes.

`action_blind` is a mandatory ablation that receives the same causal state
history but replaces future p04/p07 issued action with the active nominal.
It cannot be selected.  Neural capacity, seeds, optimizer, epoch budget and
selection rules are fixed before the first fit; no larger network is launched
after seeing a fold.

## Normalization and gates

Absolute errors are reported in physical units.  Paired response error uses
fixed scales `[0.1 mm, 0.1 mm, 25 A]` and is the aggregate scaled L2 error
divided by aggregate scaled target L2.  The action-blind paired-response
NRMSE is therefore the explicit zero-response comparator, normally one.

A deterministic candidate is eligible only if all of the following hold:

- every fold rolling one-step p95 absolute error is at most
  `[0.5 mm, 0.5 mm, 50 A]`;
- every fold free-recursive p95 absolute error is at most
  `[2.0 mm, 2.0 mm, 100 A]`;
- every fold paired-response NRMSE is below `0.95`, and the three-fold mean
  is at most `0.75`;
- at least 27/36 action cells have positive predicted/true R/Z peak-vector
  cosine, with at least 8/12 in each held context;
- it improves mean paired-response NRMSE by at least 20% over the
  action-blind comparator.

The probabilistic ensemble is additionally eligible only if its held-context
90% marginal interval coverage is at least 80% for each output and its
maximum p95 half width is no more than `[1.0 mm, 1.0 mm, 75 A]`.  These are
development gates, not conformal calibration.

Among eligible candidates, select the lowest three-fold mean paired-response
NRMSE.  A candidate within 5% of the best is treated as tied, and the fixed
simplicity order `stable_lpv`, `small_gru`, `causal_tcn`,
`probabilistic_ensemble` selects the simpler member.  The selected family is
then refit on all 39 unique development cells and emitted with full feature,
normalization, source-evidence and code fingerprints.  No held-context metric
may be recomputed after changing the candidate.

## Routes and authorization

- evidence, raw, causality or forbidden-read failure:
  `ONE_MS_ID2G1_INPUT_OR_DATA_INTEGRITY_FAIL_NO_MODEL`;
- deterministic training/recomputation failure:
  `ONE_MS_ID2G1_TRAINING_OR_REPRODUCIBILITY_FAIL_STOP`;
- no eligible candidate:
  `ONE_MS_ID2G1_GROUPED_MODEL_FAIL_ROUTE_REVIEW`;
- eligible selected model:
  `ONE_MS_ID2G1_GROUPED_MODEL_PASS_FRESH_CALIBRATION_DESIGN_ONLY`.

A PASS authorizes only a separately frozen fresh calibration design followed
by an unopened whole-context/history holdout design after model and feature
hashes are fixed.  It does not authorize ID-2C2 access, a controller, MPC,
uncertainty tube, recovery, transport/crossing, online adaptation, expert
data, BC/DAgger or RL.  A FAIL stops network enlargement and requires route
review rather than changing these gates.
