# Fixed-1000 D2 history-conditioned candidate-value result

D2 completed all 12 authentic trajectories and all 576 verified plant
advances. Ten primary trajectories carry prospectively declared development
weight; two exact semantic replays carry zero fit weight. A structurally
independent audit rebuilt all 588 raw states, 576 issued actions and 564
observed-slew transitions and reproduced the primary scientific result.

Both conditioner histories passed. With even-plus history, the best h4
two-axis matrix had condition `1.51218` and sigma-min `0.213163 mm`; with
odd-plus history the corresponding values were `1.54009` and `0.208018 mm`.
At h8, even responses were about `0.29--0.31 mm` and odd responses about
`0.90--0.91 mm`. Signal, signed separation, geometry, Ip and return-tail
gates all passed.

D2 proves only that the finite four-candidate response/value labels survive
these two complete histories. It does not prove arbitrary-history
generalization, a point plant model, Authority, capture, hold, Recourse or
feedback control. The next bounded step is a conservative direct
candidate-response set/ranking model, followed—only if development passes—by
fresh calibration and unopened blind histories.
