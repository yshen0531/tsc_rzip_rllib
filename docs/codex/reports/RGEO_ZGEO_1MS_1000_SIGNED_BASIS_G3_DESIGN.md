# Fixed-1000 signed Card15 action-basis G3 design

## Purpose

G2R1 cleanly rejected the even/odd integer lattice and its radial policy. G3
is the single bounded action-basis successor. It does not tune G2's vertical
scale, switch issue, level depth or scientific gates. It asks whether four
materially different, exactly quantized Card15 sign coordinates produce a
persistent two-dimensional task-plane cone at more than one causal phase.

## Frozen input basis

At the exact 1000-ms q0 command, each axis uses a requested single-turn
amplitude of `0.14 A`, held for issues `[phase, phase+3]`, then returns exactly
to q0. The four TSC-order sign codes are:

- `even`: `++++++++++++++`;
- `odd`: `+++---+-+-+-+-`;
- `block2`: `++--++--++--++`;
- `block4`: `++++----++++--`.

The basis was selected before new plant response solely by exact Card15 input
geometry. At the source it has executed rank four, condition `1.4383753534`
and maximum absolute component `0.14583333333333334 A`. `block2` and `block4`
are materially different from G2's two coordinates; they are not learned from
G2 outcome data.

## Campaign and roles

Phases are issues 8 and 24. All four axes and both signs are measured at each
phase: 16 primary development rollouts. `p24_block2_plus` and
`p24_block4_minus` are repeated only for integrity. Total budget is exactly 18
resets / 720 one-ms advances, horizon state1000--1040, no retry. The inherited
authentic B0 q0 trajectory is the matched response baseline. Primary rows are
prospectively fit-eligible; the two replays have zero fit weight. G2R1 and all
qualification/evaluation rows remain zero-fit.

## Gates

Every rollout must pass exact source, paired-boundary, Card15, issued and
observed slew, absolute-current, limiter, Ip, raw and timing gates. Each signed
arm must retain at least `0.03 mm` R/Z norm at h4 or h8; each signed pair must
separate by at least `0.06 mm`; h4/h8 absolute Ip response must remain below
`200 A`.

At each phase, either h4 or h8 must provide all of:

- every one of the four odd-response columns has norm at least `0.03 mm`;
- the eight signed odd vectors have maximum angular gap no larger than
  `180 deg`;
- over 64 task-plane directions, the weakest best projection is at least
  `0.02 mm`;
- the 2x4 odd-response matrix has rank two and condition at most `20`.

Only h4/h8 values count; a single-frame hybrid peak cannot substitute. Both
critical replays must be exact.

## Routes and stop rule

PASS is finite development/Authority geometry only. It may open one bounded
history-conditioned candidate-value model and a separate sustained-sequence
Authority/Recourse design; it does not open feedback. FAIL closes this
four-axis basis. No fifth sign code, neighboring amplitude, adjacent phase or
longer pulse may be appended; the next decision must change nominal/action
semantics or the terminal objective at architecture level.
