# ID2Z32 post-event delayed-tail D0 design

Date frozen: 2026-08-21

ID2Z32 is one fresh simulator-development campaign after the deterministic
state49/50 event.  It does not fit a model.  Its purpose is to create a
prospectively fit-eligible signed data set whose label includes the complete
available exact-return and delayed tail, rather than truncating learning and
evaluation at h8.

The matrix is a fresh transition-centre baseline, qR/qZ plus/minus branches
at issues 50 and 56, and one exact replay of issue50 qZ+.  Every branch uses
eight same-sign issues followed by the already qualified eight-issue exact
Card15 bridge.  All eight non-replay branches have fit weight one; baseline
and replay have zero weight.  Siblings remain a whole-history family in all
future splits.

Budget is ten resets, 730 advance attempts/gotsc calls/verified advances,
740 states and 3,700 raw artifacts.  Retry and post-result additions are
forbidden.  Phase50 finishes its bridge at effect state66 and retains seven
states of tail; phase56 finishes at effect state72 and retains state73.  The
entire state suffix after each issue is part of the future model target; safe
complete rows are not reduced to their h4/h8 summaries.

The frozen D0 signal gates retain the earlier finite requirements: every
branch must have at least 0.05/0.10 mm RZ response at h4/h8, h4--h8 cosine at
least 0.5, paired Ip at most 350 A, and both phase/horizon signed sets must
have maximum angular gap at most 180 degrees and weakest-best projection at
least 0.02 mm.  Exact replay, exact prefix/action/return, raw integrity and
hard simulator-development safety gates are mandatory.  Delayed one-step R
events above +0.20 mm from state49 through73 are recorded for every row but
are measured hybrid labels, not post-hoc exclusions.

A clean D0 PASS authorizes only bounded event-aware/set-valued model design
using the eight development families, followed by new calibration and blind
whole-history validation.  D0 FAIL closes this post-event q cell; no adjacent
phase/share/duration or larger-network rescue is allowed.  Neither outcome is
feedback, Authority-L0, Recourse-L1, source capture, waypoint/path or R_mid
evidence.
