## 1. Scientific boundary

R8R14 authenticated 112 new constant-direction cumulative staircase
trajectories and completed the eight-member direction/sign atlas. Every
runtime, restart/source-prefix, causality, calibration, Card15, action,
current, finite-state, raw, report, and formal-evaluator integrity gate passed,
but every fixed family remained at `5/16` or `6/16` formal coverage and
repaired `0/10` failed baselines. Its frozen route is:

```text
CUMULATIVE_MULTIDIRECTION_ATLAS_AUTHORITY_INSUFFICIENT_SEQUENCE_BASIS_REDESIGN_REQUIRED
```

Among the ten failed contexts, direction-2 positive was the best measured
constant staircase in six, while direction-1 negative was best in four. The
best gains were positive in all ten but never crossed zero. This contrast
supports one bounded new question: can changing between those two measured
coordinates over the four decision times produce real sequence authority that
no constant sequence exposed?

R8R15 does not claim that either coordinate is intrinsically a transport or
braking mode. It does not fit a superposition model or treat a held oracle as
a causal selector. It measures a prospectively fixed temporal basis through
real TSC.

## 2. Immutable sources

R8R15 must authenticate, without modifying or rerunning:

1. the 16 accepted R8R7 baselines and exact restart payload/snapshot lineage;
2. the final R8R12 v2 direction-2-positive constant staircase (`UUUU`);
3. the final R8R14 direction-1-negative constant staircase (`VVVV`), all 112
   R8R14 raw inventories, final state/manifest/report, and compact evidence;
4. the exact package and formal-timing lineage already authenticated by those
   stages.

The symbols are fixed as:

```text
U = canonical direction 2, sign +1, scale 1.0
V = canonical direction 1, sign -1, scale 1.0
decision task steps = [10, 14, 18, 22]
```

`UUUU` and `VVVV` are immutable measured controls. They are reused in the
formal atlas and must not be rerun.

## 3. Prospective temporal basis

Exactly eight new globally fixed sequences are allowed:

```text
candidate  step10  step14  step18  step22  code
0          U       V       V       V       UVVV
1          U       U       V       V       UUVV
2          U       U       U       V       UUUV
3          V       U       U       U       VUUU
4          V       V       U       U       VVUU
5          V       V       V       U       VVVU
6          U       V       U       V       UVUV
7          V       U       V       U       VUVU
```

Candidates 0--5 cover every single switch point in both temporal orders.
Candidates 6--7 are the two alternating controls. No additional binary code,
direction, sign, scale, decision time, context-specific schedule, or
post-result family expansion is allowed under R8R15.

At every decision, the controller constructs a new exact Card15 target from
the current causal coil-current readback and the symbol's fixed coordinate.
It refreshes that stored target at every intervening task step through the
next decision or the unchanged final horizon. There is no accumulated final
cancellation. The inherited R17 controller executes only the exact source
prefix through task step 9 and is forbidden afterward.

The candidate code is a fixed experiment input. Pair/history labels, source
outcomes, future actions, future measurements, hidden wire state, and
unavailable target state are forbidden controller inputs.

## 5. Physical phases and fail-closed execution

All 16 accepted R8R7 contexts are used. The first two ordered pairs, both
histories, form safety; the remaining six pairs form qualification:

```text
safety          4 contexts * 8 sequences = 32 trajectories
qualification  12 contexts * 8 sequences = 96 trajectories
maximum new authentic trajectories       = 128
```

Qualification is unauthorized until all 32 safety raw files pass primary and
independent runtime, full-horizon, restart/source-prefix, calibration,
sequence-event, exact-target-chain, Card15, action, current, finite-state,
forbidden-input, strict-JSON, and inventory agreement. Formal outcomes remain
closed during both physical phases.

At runtime every issue and refresh rechecks the unchanged action, current,
cosine, off-basis, Card15, saturation, and finite-state gates before the next
plant advance. A failed candidate is rejected before its action and before any
later plant step. Any runtime, source-prefix, causality, action, current, raw,
or primary/independent disagreement stops the campaign as:

```text
BINARY_TEMPORAL_SWITCHING_STAIRCASE_EXECUTION_OR_SAFETY_FAIL_STOP
```

The safety/qualification partition may be resumed only for missing specs when
the package, controller semantics, experiment identity, frozen spec matrix,
and source fingerprints are unchanged. No completed trajectory may be rerun.

The unchanged contract is:

```text

slew 0.9:       arrive <= 270 ms, hold/evaluate through 370 ms
R/Z tolerance 30 mm; speed threshold 0.1 m/s
```

The final measured atlas contains, for every context:

```text

```

Both formal paths must reproduce every source/new result with absolute
tolerance `1e-12`, and primary/independent candidate summaries and numerics
must agree exactly. The do-nothing-safe held oracle chooses among the baseline
and ten measured sequences only for evaluation. It is not a deployable causal
selector.

```text

at least one failed baseline repaired by R8R15     >= 1/10
held measured oracle formal pass count             >= 7/16

```

PASS route:

```text
BINARY_TEMPORAL_SWITCHING_STAIRCASE_PASS_CAUSAL_SELECTOR_DESIGN_REQUIRED
```

FAIL route:


A PASS authorizes only a separately frozen visible-state causal selector or

robustness claims, expert data, imitation learning, or residual RL.

## 7. Scope and learning prohibition

R8R15 is a finite real sequence-identification sentinel, not MPC and not Gate
A. It creates no new snapshot and must not rerun R8, R8R1, R8R12, or R8R14.
Every R8R15 trajectory and every R8-family source trajectory is probe/control-
development evidence and is forbidden from expert, BC, DAgger, and RL data.

No arrival deadline, hold endpoint, tolerance, safety cap, causality field,
source fingerprint, sequence family, partition, or scientific threshold may
be changed after an outcome is observed. Gate A and all learning remain
blocked regardless of R8R15 outcome.
