## 1. Scientific boundary

R8R15 physically measured ten of the sixteen four-slot binary sequences over
the same sixteen authentic restart contexts. Every measured sequence passed
exact execution and integrity but preserved the baseline formal partition at
`6/16`. R8R16--R8R19 then tested prospectively frozen affine, nonlinear, and
complete Boolean-kernel predictors. Each reproduced all formal
classifications, but each failed its unchanged held numerical gate. R8R19's
final route is:

```text
SATURATED_BOOLEAN_KERNEL_MODEL_INADEQUATE_DIRECT_CUBE_COMPLETION_REDESIGN_REQUIRED
```

R8R20 asks exactly one bounded physical question: do any of the six still
unmeasured binary sequences repair at least one of the ten failed contexts
when executed through the already authenticated R8R15 action semantics?

R8R20 does not fit, select, or validate another response predictor. It does
not infer a causal sequence selector from a held oracle, execute MPC, or
claim continuous-action or global plant authority.

   independent raw audits;
2. the exact 16 accepted R8R7 restart contexts and source-prefix lineage
   authenticated by R8R15;
3. the immutable R8R12 `UUUU`, R8R14 `VVVV`, and eight R8R15 mixed-sequence
   physical results;

The symbols and physical controller boundary are unchanged from R8R15:

```text

decision task steps = [10, 14, 18, 22]

```

At every decision, the controller constructs a fresh exact Card15 target
from the current causal coil-current readback and the selected symbol's fixed
coordinate. It refreshes that stored target at every intervening task step
through the next decision or frozen horizon. No accumulated final
cancellation is added. Future state, future actions, hidden wire state,
unavailable target state, source outcomes, and held-oracle labels remain
forbidden controller inputs.
