# R_geo/Z_geo 1 ms ID-2Z11 bounded capture teacher design

Date: 2026-08-19 Asia/Shanghai

## Purpose

ID-2Z11 is one finite, truth-recentered exact-TSC branch teacher. It answers a
single control-relevant question: can a prospectively frozen, source-local
signed action grammar produce and exactly replay a six-state capture seed from
the authenticated state-49 corridor, while retaining useful behavior under a
different state-53 arrival history?

It is not a learned model, online Oracle, controller, recovery policy,
Recourse-L1 result, waypoint result or reachability proof.

## Evidence and correction from ID-2Z9/10

ID-2Z9's exact teacher reduced the state-49 hold score from `4.407678` to
`2.983667`, but the selected five-macro path ended at `26.767972 mm` and
`0.2983667 m/s`. ID-2Z10 then rejected both frozen response models: both chose
`b4` in every fold and neither emitted an artifact. The next discriminator
therefore precedes another model and changes the objective from paired
position response to actual capture and continuation margins.

The five ID-2Z10 roots are nested states on one lineage. ID-2Z11 adds a
prospectively constructed non-nested state-53 arrival history instead of
calling adjacent states independent history support.

## Exact action grammar

The existing exact-Card15 tokens remain:

- `H`: hold the current absolute target;
- `B`: one `p07:minus` increment;
- `F`: one `p03` forward increment.

Exactly two previously measured signed complements are added:

- `U`: one exact `p03` unwind increment, the exact signed inverse of `F`;
- `P`: one exact `p07:plus` increment, the measured signed complement of `B`.

No continuous 14-D search, clipping, post-result primitive addition or
unmeasured coordinate is allowed. The seven fixed eight-issue candidates are
`H8`, `B8`, `F8`, `U4H4`, `P4H4`, `B4F4`, and `F4B4`.

## Causal branch state machine

All rollouts reset to the authenticated 1100 ms source and replay their full
Card15 prefix. The common observation horizon is state 77.

1. At state 49, execute all seven candidate branches. Select by the frozen
   capture-first score, but commit only the first four issues of the winner.
2. Reconstruct the resulting true state-53 main history and execute the same
   seven candidates. The winner is committed in full for eight issues, then
   held to state 77.
3. Construct one non-nested state-53 history by a frozen rule: use `FFFF` if
   the main committed state-49 chunk was `BBBB`, otherwise use `BBBB`.
   Execute exactly two support branches from it: `H8` and the state-53 main
   winner. This history is support evidence only and cannot change the main
   winner.
4. Fresh-reset and exactly replay the complete selected main path. No retry
   or cleanup/control action follows a failed attempt.

This is 7 + 7 + 2 branch rollouts plus one replay: at most 17 resets, 1,309
plant advances, 1,326 retained states and 6,630 required artifacts.

## Observations, scoring and gates

Before every issue, current same-step paired-boundary `R_geo/Z_geo` and
same-step `Ip` are exact/noiseless observations. Complete post-takeover causal
state, issued/serialized/applied/readback and queue history is available.
The successor after the proposed action remains unknown before issue.

Each candidate is observed through state 77. Its terminal window is states
72--77 inclusive. The score is the maximum of:

```text
max source R/Z distance / 25 mm
max one-step R/Z speed / 0.1 m/s
max absolute source-relative Ip fraction / 5%
```

The selected main path passes scientifically only if all six terminal states
meet all three bounds. Relative score improvement is diagnostic and never
substitutes for capture.

The non-nested support path must be complete and the main winner must either
capture there or improve its score over the matched alternate hold by at
least `0.02`. This is finite support for the frozen grammar, not history
generalization.

All Card15, per-coil `<=0.3 A` slew, absolute current, limiter, paired
boundary, Ip, prefix, effect timing, runtime, raw inventory and independent
audit gates remain fail closed. The 35 mm / 7.5% simulator-development shell
and 50 mm / 10% hard envelope are distinct from the 25 mm / 5% capture set.
The empirical post-successor trip is not a pre-action tube or recovery proof.

## Storage and execution discipline

The estimated raw budget is 82 GB. Before TSC, the server must have at least
120 GB free and at least 35 GB remaining after the estimate. The output path
must not already exist. Tests, offline validation and real execution occur on
the server only. Raw is retained through independent audit and compact/hash
recovery; only then may this exact owned stage tree be deleted to recover
space, with the deletion recorded.

## Routes and stop decisions

- Input, package, storage or offline failure: zero new TSC and repair only the
  same semantics if scientifically unchanged.
- Runtime, prefix, interface or raw-integrity failure: stop, preserve raw and
  do not call it a plant or grammar result.
- No eligible root/main arm, incomplete critical branch, failed replay, main
  no-capture, or alternate support failure: the frozen grammar fails. Close
  the manual source-local macro ladder and route to an action-authority,
  nominal/takeover and reachability review. Do not add depth or a larger net.
- Full PASS: authorize only a fresh replay-confirmed capture candidate and a
  separately frozen value/reachability data/model stage. Recourse-L1 still
  requires a bounded-tube continuation/feedback qualification.

## Final-goal boundary

The final goal remains fixed-1100-ms, 1-ms exact-RZI, full-causal-history,
exact-Card15 two-axis waypoint/path control under independent hard safety and
recovery, eventually including bidirectional repeated `R_mid` crossing with
continuous belief. ID-2Z11 is only one source-local capture discriminator.
