# Post-ID-2Z23 centered co-allocation route review

Date: 2026-08-20 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Decision

The high-level new-round architecture remains valid, but the intermediate
control coordinate must change.  The next stage is one bounded zero-TSC,
zero-fit exact-Card15 redesign of a reserve-aware transition nominal and a
centered signed co-allocation cell.  It is not another full-F replacement,
adjacent phase/amplitude probe, model fit, Authority-L0 claim or Recourse
claim.

If and only if the exact lattice, cumulative closure, current, support and
storage gates pass, the result may freeze one prospective fit-eligible TSC
campaign.  That campaign must use its interior nominal as the matched
scientific baseline, retain every predeclared sibling, and separate the D0
learning-readiness verdict from sequence-level Authority-L0.  Capture remains
diagnostic for D0 and remains a mandatory stronger milestone before
Recourse-L1 qualification.

## What remains valid

- At every issue boundary the current same-step paired-boundary R_geo/Z_geo
  and same-step Ip are exact noiseless observations.
- Complete post-takeover causal observation, issued, quantized, applied,
  readback and queue history is available.  Belief and uncertainty describe
  latent memory, future response and model mismatch, not current or past RZI.
- Exact Card15 fields, issue k -> state k+1, per-coil slew <= 0.3 A, absolute
  current, Ip, boundary, queue/effect and fail-closed gates remain hard.
- Whole-history development/calibration/blind separation, independent raw
  audit, Authority and Recourse as independent claims, and fresh in-loop
  validation remain mandatory.
- Full-F p03 stride-1 remains the strongest measured finite takeover
  transport.  That fact does not make it a qualified feedback nominal.

## Earliest load-bearing route error

The p03 stride-1 source sequence was selected mainly for terminal source
distance and Ip.  It was not selected jointly for terminal velocity, symmetric
feedback reserve, moving-phase task-plane authority or terminal
recoverability.  Later work first held the issue-15 level for clean
identification and then tried late braking and hand-authored macros.  Those
experiments are valid finite evidence, but the temporary held corridor and
the saturated full-F transport were repeatedly treated as if either were a
controller-ready nominal.

ID-2Z18/ID-2Z19 also compared models before the action grammar had control-
aligned terminal authority.  Their failures correctly stopped false model
qualification, but they do not show that machine learning is unnecessary.
They show that a model cannot manufacture missing actuator reserve or learn a
useful value map from a miscentered action coordinate.

## ID-2Z23 attribution

ID-2Z23 remains final as
`ONE_MS_ID2Z23_MOVING_NOMINAL_VERTEX_GEOMETRY_FAIL_NEW_ACTION_BASIS_REQUIRED`.
Its execution, prefix, Card15, raw and replay evidence are clean.  The new
read-only decomposition changes only the next-route interpretation.

Each branch replaced F with a held-state vertex V for four issues and then
resumed F from the attained target.  It did not add a residual around F and
did not return to the baseline target path.  Relative to the full-F baseline:

- the robust replacement differences V-F are rank four with condition
  3.426911;
- their common mean accounts for 68.4114% of action-difference energy;
- the common mode accounts for 91.4112% and 89.7813% of concatenated h1--h8
  R/Z response energy at issues 24 and 32;
- after four replacements the persistent target offset has L2 magnitude
  4.5663--6.2496 A and per-coil maximum 1.8--2.4 A.

Full-F itself has per-step L-infinity 0.3 A.  Its symmetric reserve is only
about 8.33 mA on coils 1--8 and approximately 0--10 microampere on coils
9--14, while the measured p05 signed direction needs about 145.8--150 mA.
Therefore direct `full-F + residual` is not an admissible action.

The p05 pair provides the cleanest separation.  Its two issued increments are
opposite to within the source Card15/readback micro-bias.  The odd response
half-difference cancels the common omitted-F term and agrees across the two
moving phases almost exactly: the h4 and h8 cross-phase direction cosines are
0.9999976 and 0.9999991.  It also agrees with the old held-state odd direction
with cosine 0.9963--0.9999 and gain 1.046--1.099.  Most other inter-vertex
contrasts likewise transport; p00 alone shows a genuine history-dependent
late event.

The correct conclusion is therefore:

> full-F-centered replacement geometry failed, while at least the p05 signed
> differential and most inter-vertex differential structure survived.  The
> dominant blocker is nominal centering and slew allocation, with a separate
> history/event issue for p00.  This is not global action-basis or plant
> unreachability.

## Proposed exact-lattice preflight

The preflight must search a frozen finite Decimal/Card15 lattice, not copy a
floating-point half-F vector.  Its deterministic objective is:

1. retain the largest admissible share of F in a finite transition/deceleration
   nominal;
2. subject to exact symmetric signed residual cells, exact cumulative
   zero-residual closure, current/headroom limits and a bounded input
   condition;
3. preserve a preregistered minimum residual scale on at least two and
   preferably three independent actuator directions;
4. never use clipping or future plant values.

A continuous seed is `N ~= 0.5 F` with residual directions proportional to
`0.5 p00-minus`, `1.0 p05-plus` and `0.5 p06-plus`.  In unquantized
single-turn amperes, the six N +/- r points fit the 0.3 A cube and the three
directions have condition about 1.163.  This is nomination evidence only.
The already executed ID-2Z15 exact `f50` is not the arithmetic half-F target;
one coil differs by about 0.05 A and one naive centered candidate would reach
about 0.349985 A.  Exact source/current-specific construction is therefore a
hard pre-TSC gate.

The interior nominal is not a new full-horizon constant fraction.  Early
full-F transport may be retained, followed by a prospectively chosen finite
transition/deceleration window with reserve.  The switch rule must be based on
pre-result distance, velocity, current reserve and viability criteria; the old
issue-15 hold is not a default switch point.  ID-2Z15's constant f50/duty
failure remains valid and is not reopened.

## Prospective campaign semantics

If the preflight passes, one separately frozen campaign must include:

- a matched interior-nominal baseline and a zero-weight full-F cost/transport
  diagnostic;
- balanced, zero-net signed Card15 codes around the same nominal, with exact
  residual closure and a common long tail;
- multiple predeclared phases and complete causal-history families, without
  after-result phase, duration or amplitude additions;
- complete h1--h8 onset, sustained, sign-reversal/return and terminal-velocity
  observations;
- prospective development-fit eligibility for every complete non-replay
  sibling, zero fit weight for replays, and frozen future calibration/blind
  whole-history families.

Two gates are intentionally separate:

- **D0 learning readiness:** exact execution, raw/replay integrity, action
  support, persistent distinguishable action-conditioned signal and complete
  family isolation.  Per-phase positive span and six-state capture are not D0
  prerequisites; weak, rotated and one-sided responses are valid labels.
- **Authority-L0:** fresh sequence-level evidence of sustained two-axis
  position/velocity utility, control reserve and cross-history reproduction.
  Input rank, model fit or a single-frame peak cannot pass Authority-L0.

D0 PASS may open bounded shadow model development in parallel with a separate
Authority-L0/Recourse design.  It does not open a controller.  The first real
feedback sentinel still requires fresh model calibration/blind PASS AND
Authority-L0 PASS AND Recourse-L1 PASS AND the exact hard interface.

## Model role after D0

Machine learning is not postponed until every physical mechanism is known.
It should learn the mapping

```text
exact current RZI/current/queue + compressed causal history + candidate code
  -> multi-horizon RZI increments
  -> terminal one-ms velocity/increment and viability/capture margins
  -> candidate value, uncertainty and OOD refusal
```

The bounded comparison remains one structured nominal/action-memory/value
model versus the same backbone plus one small event-aware causal residual.
No third model or capacity ladder is authorized.  TSC branch evaluation is a
slow development teacher/sequence verifier; the learned model amortizes it and
must be truth-recentered every millisecond.

## Hard stops

- Exact lattice, closure, cumulative current or static support failure: zero
  TSC; redesign the action coordinate or reduce/reconstruct the nominal.
- D0 execution, replay or overall distinguishability failure: close the whole
  centered cell; do not try adjacent 0.4/0.6 shares, phases or durations.
- D0 PASS but Authority-L0 FAIL: a shadow model may remain descriptive, but no
  controller opens; redesign the nominal/action basis.
- Both frozen model candidates fail fresh whole-history gates: no third model.
- Exact current return, hold, safe stop or replay is not state recovery or
  Recourse-L1.

## Final goal and distance

The final goal remains fixed-1100-ms takeover and one-ms causal control using
exact current R_geo/Z_geo/Ip and complete post-takeover history, under exact
Card15, 0.3 A slew, current/Ip, queue/effect and independent recovery gates, to
approximately follow finite two-axis relative displacements, waypoints and
paths.  Later qualification must cover multiple positions and histories,
return motion and repeated bidirectional R_mid crossing without resetting
belief.

The current route has exact observation/interface semantics and finite
transport evidence, but still lacks moving-nominal sustained two-axis
authority, capture/hold, Recourse-L1, a fresh-qualified history model, any real
feedback sentinel, multi-position coverage or R_mid crossing.  The project is
still establishing the right control coordinate and prospective learning
distribution; it is not close to final control qualification.
