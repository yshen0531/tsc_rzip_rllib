focused, full-suite and zero-TSC offline gates.  Its one authorized execution

a scientific branch FAIL and not a TSC/runtime/interface failure.

## Permitted repair

The repair may only:

1. map the descriptive helper's legacy inner key to ID-2Z6's already frozen
   `simulator_development_preissue_clearance` object;
2. authenticate all five immutable round-0 compact/raw paths, actions,
   artifacts and exact state-49 prefix without a plant advance;
3. recompute the unchanged frozen round score and selection;
4. continue at round 1 without replaying any round-0 path; and
5. record both the original experiment revision and the distinct hotfix
   revision in the final primary and independent evidence.

The adapter values are descriptive outputs only.  They do not enter branch
eligibility, ordering, action construction, capture, safe-stop, storage,
budget or final utility gates.  The config and its SHA-256 remain unchanged.

## Mandatory resume preflight

Before any further TSC call, ID-2Z6R1 must independently reparse all 350 raw
states and hash all 1,750 required artifacts.  It must verify the exact five
round-0 IDs, 69 actions and 70 states per path, original source revision,
zero-TSC original offline result, exact W2 prefix, no existing final result,
no previous resume identity, sufficient storage and unchanged frozen action
streams.  The deterministic recomputation must select an eligible round-0
arm.  Any failure stops with zero additional plant advances.

If the preflight passes, it writes one immutable resume metadata record and
continues only rounds 1 and 2 plus the already frozen zero-fit critical
replay.  Round 0 is never rerun.  All original total caps remain 16 resets,
1,104 attempts/calls/verified advances, 1,120 states and 5,600 artifacts.

## Evidence and claim boundary

The final independent audit must reparse the entire combined raw tree,
reconstruct every Card15 action, recompute all prefixes, rounds, selection,
inventory, counters, route and scientific metrics, and additionally bind the
resume metadata and hotfix revision.

ID-2Z6's route meanings do not change.  A PASS remains only finite
source-local teacher utility plus prospective complete-window development
readiness; it may authorize fresh replay/Recourse-L1 design and a separately
frozen small-model comparison.  A no-utility result closes this exact B/F/H
route.  Neither result is hold, recovery, controller, waypoint, crossing or
global reachability evidence.
