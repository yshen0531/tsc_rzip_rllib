# R_geo/Z_geo 1 ms post-ID-2E1 learning-route review

Date: 2026-08-17 Asia/Shanghai

## Decision

ID-2E1 remains final as
`ONE_MS_ID2E1_GROUPED_DEVELOPMENT_MODEL_FAIL_ROUTE_REVIEW`. It rejected a
fixed-active-nominal, action-history-only convolution family; it did not
reject a measurement-recentred history-conditioned model. ID-2C2 was not
opened and remains evaluator-only.

The route is amended so that a complete physical explanation of every
state19/state27 excursion is not a prerequisite for machine learning.
"Causal" has two distinct meanings here:

1. the mandatory predictor contract uses only quantities available before
   the predicted successor, with exact issue/effect timing and no future
   leakage;
2. a mechanistic explanation of every TSC or boundary branch is useful
   diagnostics, but is not required before fitting a conditional predictive
   distribution.

Only the first is a model-development prerequisite. Raw boundary checks are
retained as a compact integrity gate; they must not expand into an unbounded
mechanism-forensics programme.

## Evidence boundary

The four ID-2E1 p04/p07 candidates received virtual residual-action history
and, for one candidate, a linear absolute-time interaction. They did not
receive the current exact R_geo/Z_geo/Ip, actual-current innovation or a
compressed causal belief. Their failure therefore rejects that open-loop
smooth residual class rather than the planned one-ms measurement-recentred
architecture.

The isolated minus-sign excursions at states 19 and 27 are authentic
baseline-relative records under the current paired-boundary contract, but
each action cell has only one realization. They may be nonlinear or
multimodal training targets without first being assigned a physical cause.
Conversely, p09's development NRMSE of about 0.172 was an in-sample fit of
two arms, not grouped event generalization.

The p03-minus active nominal remains useful as a source-local identification
centre: fresh evidence showed about 34.19% terminal R/Z drift reduction.
It is not Nominal-H1, a hold, a recovery policy or a controller-grade
corridor.

## Next data identity

The next stage shall be a prospectively frozen, fit-eligible TSC development
campaign around the held p03-minus active nominal. It shall use three whole
causal contexts:

- no conditioning probe;
- one p04-plus conditioning issue at issue 18 followed by exact return;
- one p07-plus conditioning issue at issue 18 followed by exact return.

Within each context it shall include its own no-probe baseline and p04/p07
issue-22 residual probes over both signs and durations 1, 2 and 4 issues.
Every unique whole-history cell shall have two fresh replays. This gives 39
unique cells and 78 trajectories. Siblings and replays remain atomic in
all later splits. The issue-18 conditioners avoid duplicating ID-2C2's
issue-16 one-issue evaluator cells.

The new raw may become development-fit data only after execution, Card15,
slew/current/Ip, paired-boundary integrity, complete-tail, repeatability and
action-support gates pass. It is forbidden for calibration, blind holdout,
controller safety, expert/Oracle/BC/DAgger/RL or fixtures. The stage shall
not force a state19/state27 event to occur; zero, continuous and jump
responses are all legitimate learning targets.

## Model stage after a data PASS

The first model comparison shall predict

```text
p(delta R_geo, delta Z_geo, delta Ip over the horizon
  | current exact R_geo/Z_geo/Ip,
    causal observed/current/action history,
    candidate issued Card15 actions)
```

It shall compare, under identical whole-history folds:

1. a stable low-order/state-space or LPV baseline;
2. a small GRU;
3. a causal TCN;
4. a probabilistic mixture or ensemble output able to cover isolated jumps.

The previous action-only fixed-pole model remains an ablation, not the
mandatory backbone. Evaluation must distinguish rolling one-step error,
recursive free rollout, smooth-response error, jump/event coverage, Ip and
uncertainty calibration. A jump need not be mechanistically explained, but
it may not be deleted or hidden by an average prediction.

ID-2C2 remains unopened during data design and model selection. Fresh
calibration and a blind whole-context/history holdout are separate future
identities created only after the model family and features are frozen.

## Control-relevance gate

A predictive PASS does not imply authority. Before controller TSC, the
route must separately establish finite active hold/drift suppression,
two-sided R/Z authority, reverse and remaining Ip/current margin, using
bounded TSC shooting or an equivalent prospectively frozen discriminator.
Only calibrated uncertainty plus qualified recourse may support constrained
rolling control.

The eventual goal remains fixed-1100-ms, one-ms, safe causal two-axis
relative/path/waypoint tracking over a declared finite domain, including
both directions and repeated R_mid crossings with continuous belief and Ip
safety.

## Stop conditions

Pause for route review rather than enlarging the network if any of these
occurs:

- the new whole-history cells are not reproducible enough to calibrate a
  useful conditional distribution;
- identical allowed causal prefixes and actual action paths produce a
  successor split wider than the permitted uncertainty envelope;
- GRU/TCN/mixture candidates do not improve fresh whole-family evaluation
  over the simplest causal baseline;
- calibrated uncertainty is too wide for any useful action;
- bounded shooting cannot find finite hold, two-sided motion and safe
  reverse under the declared action grammar;
- recourse or an end-to-end paired-boundary/fail-closed controller port
  cannot be qualified.

This review itself changes no controller, reward, termination, Card15,
queue, TSC state or data semantics.
